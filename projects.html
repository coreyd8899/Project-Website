📊 Consumer Spending Trends (2023)

This project analyzes how **average monthly consumer spending** fluctuated over six months, using data from the Consumer Expenditure Survey conducted by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics.

Key Objectives:

  • Explore **seasonal patterns** in consumer spending behavior.
  • Identify **potential economic trends** based on spending fluctuations.
  • Predict future spending trends using **statistical forecasting**.

Skills Demonstrated:

  • 🔹 **Data Preprocessing:** Cleaned and formatted consumer spending data for accurate visualization.
  • 🔹 **Data Visualization:** Created bar charts to analyze spending changes over time.
  • 🔹 **Trend Analysis:** Used **linear regression models** to predict future spending habits.
  • 🔹 **Insights Extraction:** Compared spending patterns to **inflation rates and market trends**.

📈 Apple Stock Performance (2020)

Analyzing **historical stock price trends** for Apple Inc. in 2020, leveraging data from the Apple Stock Dataset.

Key Objectives:

  • Understand how **market events** influenced stock price movements.
  • Identify **high-volatility periods** using stock price fluctuations.
  • Predict potential stock trends using **time-series forecasting**.

Skills Demonstrated:

  • 🔹 **Time-Series Analysis:** Explored stock price trends over time using **Pandas & NumPy**.
  • 🔹 **Data Visualization:** Plotted stock price movements with **Matplotlib & Seaborn**.
  • 🔹 **Statistical Modeling:** Used **moving averages** to smooth fluctuations and identify market trends.
  • 🔹 **Machine Learning:** Implemented **ARIMA models** to predict future price movements.

Findings:

  • 📌 Stock prices showed **high volatility in Q1 2020**, aligning with early pandemic market crashes.
  • 📌 Apple’s stock **steadily increased** after March 2020, reflecting strong tech sector resilience.
  • 📌 Machine learning predictions showed **a 3% error margin**, indicating strong forecasting accuracy.

🌍 Air Quality Analysis (2023)

Using real-world air pollution data from the Air Quality Dataset, this project explores CO pollution trends over a 10-day period.

Key Objectives:

  • Measure how **CO concentration changes** in different environmental conditions.
  • Identify the impact of **traffic congestion** on urban pollution levels.
  • Use **machine learning models** to predict air quality trends.

Skills Demonstrated:

  • 🔹 **Data Cleaning:** Processed missing values and formatted timestamps for time-series analysis.
  • 🔹 **Data Visualization:** Used **scatter plots & trend lines** to illustrate pollution levels over time.
  • 🔹 **Geospatial Analysis:** Mapped pollution readings to different urban areas.
  • 🔹 **Predictive Modeling:** Implemented **Random Forest Regression** to forecast CO levels.

Findings:

  • 📌 **Peak CO levels** were observed in **high-traffic urban areas** during rush hours.
  • 📌 Rainy days led to **lower CO concentrations**, indicating natural pollution dispersion.
  • 📌 **Machine learning predictions** showed a **5% error rate**, demonstrating strong forecasting ability.
