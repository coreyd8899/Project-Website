Bridging the Digital Divide

Motivation: After working hands-on with the elderly, I saw firsthand the struggles they face with technology. This research explores how to make digital services more inclusive and accessible.

🔍 Research Questions

  • What are the main barriers preventing seniors from engaging with technology?
  • How can AI and machine learning improve digital accessibility?
  • What UX/UI improvements can enhance usability for older adults?

📊 Key Findings

Identified four major challenges: poor UX design, cognitive overload, lack of personalization, and fear of technology.

💡 Proposed Solutions

  • AI-powered adaptive UI for automatic text size and layout adjustments.
  • Voice-assisted navigation for hands-free interaction.
  • Low-tech design improvements (larger buttons, simple forms, high-contrast text).
  • Community-based digital literacy workshops.
